### 2.4 Packing essentials (hiking-specific)
- Trail shoes with grip (Sorapis has polished slippery rock)
- Light rain shell + warm mid-layer (mountain temps drop fast above 2,000 m)
- 2L water per person per hike
- Sunscreen + hat (high UV at altitude)
- Cash (€100 split across days) — many rifugios card-only is unreliable
- Trekking poles (recommended for Sassolungo-area or any descent)
- Headlamp (bonus if you ever miscalculate cable car closing)
- Italian/German basic phrases — South Tyrol is bilingual

### 2.5 Driving prep
- Italian highway tolls (A22, A27): take ticket at entry, pay credit card at exit
- Speed cameras are aggressive in villages — cruise control at the limit
- ZTL (limited traffic zones) in town centers: park outside the centro storico
- 2026 Olympics signage in Cortina — center will have detours

#### Hike: Tre Cime di Lavaredo loop
- **AllTrails:** `https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/veneto/tre-cime-di-lavaredo-drei-zinnen`
- **Distance:** 10.3 km (6.3 mi) loop
- **Elevation gain:** 425 m (1,617 ft)
- **Time:** 4–5h moving (allow 5–6h with breaks)
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (4.7★, 6,244 reviews)
- **Trailhead:** Rifugio Auronzo, 46.6122, 12.2962
- **Direction:** Counter-clockwise (101 → 104 → 102 → 105 → 101) is the standard. Clockwise has steeper ascent at the end but escapes crowds at trailhead.

#### Trailhead parking — Rifugio Auronzo
- **Address:** Località Forcella Longeres, Auronzo di Cadore, BL
- **Cost:** €40 / 12 hours
- **Booking:** mandatory online, opens 30 days before at `https://pass.auronzo.info`
- **Capacity:** ~700 cars, books up early in summer
- **Alternative:** Bus 444 from Toblach/Dobbiaco or Bus 30/31 + 50 from Cortina via Misurina (online booking required for shuttle 444 too)
- **Drive from Baita Fraina:** 47 min, 30 km via SR48 → SS48bis → toll road
- **Toll road opens:** ~24/7 in high summer; check `auronzo.info` for current hours

#### Route highlights
1. **Trail 101 from Auronzo to Lavaredo** — flat-ish military road along south face. Big views west of Cadini group.
2. **Forcella Lavaredo (2,454 m)** — saddle where the north faces appear. THE postcard view of the three vertical walls.
3. **Rifugio Locatelli area** — best photographs from a small bump 50 m above the rifugio. Optional via ferrata "Torre di Toblin" climbs steep stairs to a 2,617 m viewpoint (B/C grade — only with full ferrata kit).
4. **Trail 105 return** — gentler grass slopes, fewer people. Passes Malga Langalm (occasional summer cheese-makers).

#### Food on the trail
- **Rifugio Lavaredo** (~30 min in): drinks, snacks, restrooms
- **Rifugio Locatelli** (~3h in): full meals, strudel, knödel, beer. Cash preferred, cards work
- **Malga Langalm** (~4h in): rustic alpine pasture hut, summer only

#### Hazards & tips
- Wind on the saddle can be brutal — even in July. Bring a windproof layer.
- The loop is at 2,200–2,500 m altitude — you may feel slight breathlessness if not acclimatized.
- Crowds: arrive at 07:00 to be ahead of the day-trippers. By 10:30 the trail is busy.
- Dogs allowed on leash.

#### Bad weather option
- If thunderstorms forecast above 2,500 m: do the shorter Lavaredo Saddle out-and-back (Auronzo → Lavaredo → Forcella → return = 6 km, 2.5h)
- If full-day rain: swap with Day 3 (Sorapis is shorter and more sheltered initially)

#### Hike: Lake Sorapis via Passo Tre Croci
- **AllTrails:** `https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/veneto/passo-tre-croci-lago-sorapis`
- **Distance:** 12.7 km (7.9 mi) out-and-back
- **Elevation gain:** 600 m (1,961 ft) — note: undulating, not all in one climb
- **Time:** 4–5h moving; allow 5–6h with lake time
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (4.7★, 4,585 reviews) — but reviewers consistently warn this is *not* easy
- **Trailhead:** Passo Tre Croci, 46.5578, 12.2036

#### Trailhead parking — Passo Tre Croci
- **Cost:** Free roadside
- **Capacity:** Very small, ~20 cars at trailhead. Fills up by 09:00 weekends.
- **Backup parking:** "Parcheggio per Sorapis" lot ~1.5 km below adds 30 min walk uphill
- **No advance booking needed**
- **Drive from Baita Fraina:** 19 min, 14 km via SR48

#### Route highlights
1. **Forest entry** — first 20–30 min through pleasant pine
2. **Cable-assisted ledges** (3 sections, longest ~30 m): metal cables on rock face, narrow path, drop-off below. NOT technically difficult but bad for vertigo.
3. **Final approach to Vandelli** — opens up to alpine basin
4. **Lago di Sorapis** — milky turquoise from glacial silt; color most vivid in late morning/early afternoon
5. **Dito di Dio (Finger of God)** — ~50 m rock spire above the lake

#### Food on the trail
- **Rifugio Vandelli** (only stop): full meals, knödel, polenta. Ladin/South Tyrolean cuisine. Open ~09:00–17:00 in summer.

#### Hazards & tips
- Polished rock from years of foot traffic = slippery when wet
- Cables are well-maintained but trust them, don't lean
- "If you have any vertigo issue, this is borderline" — common review feedback
- Trail 216/213 loop variant via Forcella Marcoira: **DO NOT TAKE** unless you're an experienced alpine hiker — reviewers describe descent as "stuff of nightmares"
- Trail is busy in summer; etiquette varies — be patient at narrow passes
- Lake water level varies; can be partly dry late August

#### Bad weather option
- Wet rock makes the cable sections genuinely dangerous — swap to a different day if storms forecast
- Backup: 5 Torri area (cable car from Bai de Dones to Rifugio Scoiattoli, easy WWI museum walk)

#### Hike A: Lago di Braies lake loop
- **AllTrails:** `https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/lago-di-braies-pragser-wildsee`
- **Distance:** 3.8 km loop
- **Elevation gain:** ~30 m (essentially flat)
- **Time:** 1–1.5h
- **Difficulty:** Easy (more a walk than a hike)
- **Trailhead:** Pragser Wildsee P3, 46.6943, 12.0854

#### Hike: Seceda → Rifugio Firenze → Col Raiser loop
- **AllTrails:** `https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/seceda-rifugio-firenze-col-raiser-2`
- **Distance:** 8.5 km (5.3 mi) loop
- **Elevation gain:** 472 m (1,548 ft)
- **Time:** 3–3.5h
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (4.7★, 354 reviews)
- **Start/end:** Seceda summit cable car station, 46.5764, 11.6749
- **Direction:** Clockwise — descend first, end with the ascent back to Seceda for the views

#### Cable car — Ortisei to Seceda
- **Provider:** Seceda Cableways AG
- **Website:** `https://www.seceda.it/en`
- **Stages:** Ortisei (1,236 m) → Furnes (2,040 m) → Seceda (2,519 m); two sections, transfer at Furnes
- **Booking:** Timed entry mandatory for 2026, book online; saves 5%
- **Round-trip cost:** ~€74 (2026 prices, increased from €52 in 2025)
- **Operating hours summer:** 08:30–17:30 (last lift up varies; check)
- **Parking:** Garage Central or Parkgarage Seceda in Ortisei (€2.80/h)
- **Note:** A €5 turnstile fee applies on the main ridgeline trail

#### Route highlights
1. **Seceda summit & ridgeline viewpoint** — 15 min walk from cable car. THE Geisler/Odle peaks shot. Pay €5 turnstile if you want the prime fenced viewpoint, or skip and use alternative trails for free
2. **Descent on trail 1** — gentle slopes, alpine meadows full of wildflowers in July
3. **Rifugio Firenze (Regensburger Hütte)** — historic refuge, traditional South Tyrolean food
4. **Col Raiser plateau** — you'll see ski lift infrastructure but the views to Sassolungo are massive
5. **Climb back to Seceda** — the only "real" effort of the day (~1h, 400 m gain)

#### Food on the trail
- **Rifugio Seceda** at the top (cable car station): touristy
- **Baita Sofie** (just below summit): fancier, wine cellar, panoramic
- **Rifugio Firenze** (mid-loop): traditional dishes, knödel, goulash
- **Rifugio Fermeda** (near Col Raiser): family-friendly
- **Baita Daniel** (en route): cosy, hot chocolate

#### Hazards & tips
- The ridgeline trail can be backed up with photo-takers; arrive early (09:00 cable car)
- Wind can be strong on the ridge; pack a layer
- Don't fly drones — UNESCO area, fines apply
- The €5 turnstile is paid in cash only at the gate
- Alternative free viewpoints: walk left from the cable car station along trail 2 for 5–10 min

#### Bad weather option
- If clouds obscure the ridge (very common in July afternoons): take the cable car up briefly, see what you can, descend by lift. €74 still buys access.
- Indoor alternative: Ortisei old town, woodcarving shops, Museum Gherdëina (Ladin culture)

#### Hike: Alpe di Siusi Family Tour (RECOMMENDED)
- **AllTrails:** `https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/giro-per-famiglie-alpe-di-siusi-val-gardena`
- **Distance:** 6.6 km (4.1 mi) loop
- **Elevation gain:** 212 m (695 ft)
- **Time:** 2–2.5h
- **Difficulty:** Easy (4.7★, 691 reviews)
- **Start/end:** Compatsch / Mont Sëuc cable car top station, 46.5419, 11.6253
- **Note:** Trail starts from Compatsch — accessible from either Mont Sëuc cable car (from Ortisei) OR Seiser Alm Bahn (from Siusi/Seis am Schlern)

#### Cable car — Mont Sëuc (from Ortisei)
- **Website:** `https://www.funiviaortisei.eu`
- **Address:** Via Setil 9, Ortisei BZ
- **Hours:** 08:30–17:30 summer
- **Round-trip:** ~€30 / adult (2026 estimate)
- **Parking:** 240-space garage at valley station, paid (~€2.80/h)
- **No advance booking** required, but expect queues by 10:00 in peak summer

#### Driving access alternatives
- You CAN drive directly to Compatsch P2 if you arrive before 09:00 (road closes 09:00–17:00 to private vehicles). Cost: ~€20+ parking. Risky tight timing on a transfer day.
- Cable car from Ortisei is the most predictable for transfer day.

#### Route highlights
1. **Compatsch plateau** — opening views of Sassolungo (east), Sciliar (south), Catinaccio (west)
2. **Wildflower meadows** — peak bloom mid-June to mid-July; you'll just catch the tail end
3. **Hexenbänke (Witches' Benches)** if you take the Bullaccia variant — curious rock formations from local legend
4. **Haflinger horses** — semi-wild horses that graze the meadow

#### Food on the trail
- Multiple "Schwaige" (alpine pasture huts) all over the plateau — none more than 30 min walk from each other in this area
- Try **Gostner Schwaige** (slightly off the Family Tour route): excellent traditional South Tyrolean lunch
- **Sporthotel Sonne** if you want fancier

#### Alternative hike if you want a touch more
- **Bullaccia Tour: Witches' Benches (Hexenbänke):** 8.7 km, 380 m gain, 3–3.5h, MODERATE, 4.8★. Adds the Bullaccia plateau and the rock-bench formations. About 1 extra hour total.
- **Full original suggested route (Alpe di Siusi - Ortisei descent):** 5.5 km, mostly downhill, 2.5–3h, MODERATE — only do this if you want to descend back to Ortisei on foot rather than cable car. The slippery descent eats your knees.

#### Drive Pension Kircher Sepp → Salò
- **Distance:** 280 km
- **Time:** 3h 30m without traffic
- **Route:** Barbiano → A22 south → exit at Affi → SR450 west → Salò
- **Tolls:** ~€20 on A22
- **Lunch stops:** Trento (~halfway) is pretty if you have time; otherwise grab something in Ortisei before leaving

#### Bad weather option
- If Alpe di Siusi is fogged in: skip the hike, drive directly to Salò, arrive early afternoon, enjoy a relaxed first day at the lake
- Or: substitute a quick stop at **Lago di Carezza** (10 min loop, emerald lake with Latemar reflection, on the way south)

## 7. Weather Contingency

### Forecast sources
- `https://www.meteo.bz.it` — South Tyrol official (German/Italian/English)
- `https://www.meteotrentino.it` — Trentino official
- `Mountain-Forecast.com` for specific peaks

### Common patterns for mid-July
- Clear mornings, building cumulus by 13:00, thunderstorms 14:00–17:00 are typical
- Above 2,500 m, lightning is a real risk on exposed ridges
- Daytime temps: 20–25°C in valleys, 5–15°C at altitude

### Decision rules
- **Storms forecast above 2,500 m by 14:00:** start hikes by 06:30, be off ridges by 13:00
- **Full-day rain:** swap a hike day for indoor activities (Cortina museum, Bolzano Ötzi museum, Brixen old town, Klausen)
- **Rain during a planned hike:** Sorapis cables become genuinely dangerous when wet; Tre Cime is doable but cold; Seceda/Alpe di Siusi are fine in light rain but views are gone

### Backup activities by zone
**Cortina area:**
- 5 Torri WWI open-air museum (cable car from Bai de Dones)
- Lagazuoi cable car + WWI tunnels
- Cortina Museum of Modern Art
- Drive the Falzarego pass for car touring

**Eisacktal area:**
- Brixen old town + Hofburg cathedral
- Klausen (medieval village)
- Drive the Penser Joch pass
- Bolzano Ötzi/Iceman museum (45 min south)
- Lago di Carezza (always nice, even in light rain)
